📖 今日主题：Essential IELTS Vocabulary

今天是 8 月 29 日，让我们学习 IELTS 考试必备的高频词汇。这些词汇在听力、阅读和写作中都非常重要。

🔤 重点词汇 (Vocabulary)

address
/əˈdres/ n. & v.
n. 地址；演讲
v. 处理；解决；发表演说
名词：I live at 123 Main Street.
动词：The company addressed the problem immediately.
雅思写作：We must address the issue of climate change effectively.
consequence
/ˈkɒnsɪkwəns/ n.
n. 后果；结果
例句：The decision had serious consequences.
雅思写作：Failure to act will have dire consequences for the environment.
significant
/sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt/ adj.
adj. 重要的；有意义的；显著的
例句：This is a significant achievement.
雅思口语：The development is quite significant for our community.
particular
/pərˈtɪkjələr/ adj.
adj. 特定的；特别的
例句：I'm interested in art in particular.
雅思写作：There are certain factors that are particularly important.
frequently
/ˈfriːkwəntli/ adv.
adv. 频繁地；常常
例句：I visit my parents frequently.
雅思口语：People frequently use smartphones for entertainment.

💡 实用短语 (Useful Phrases)

in other words
换句话说
He was very angry, in other words, furious.
in addition to
除……之外
In addition to English, she speaks French fluently.
It is said that...
据说……
It is said that exercise is good for health.
on the one hand...
一方面……
On the one hand, it costs money; on the other hand, it saves time.
