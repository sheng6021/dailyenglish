🔤 重点词汇 (20 Words)

address
/əˈdres/ n. & v.
n. 地址；演讲
v. 处理；解决；发表演说
例句：I live at 123 Main Street.
雅思写作：We must address the issue of climate change effectively.
consequence
/ˈkɒnsɪkwəns/ n.
n. 后果；结果
例句：The decision had serious consequences.
雅思写作：Failure to act will have dire consequences for the environment.
significant
/sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt/ adj.
adj. 重要的；有意义的；显著的
例句：This is a significant achievement.
雅思写作：The development is quite significant for our community.
particular
/pərˈtɪkjələr/ adj.
adj. 特定的；特别的
例句：I'm interested in art in particular.
雅思写作：There are certain factors that are particularly important.
frequently
/ˈfriːkwəntli/ adv.
adv. 频繁地；常常
例句：I visit my parents frequently.
雅思写作：People frequently use smartphones for entertainment.
sustain
/səˈsteɪn/ v.
v. 维持；支撑；承受
例句：The bridge can sustain heavy traffic.
雅思写作：Economies struggle to sustain growth during crises.
implement
/ˈɪmplɪment/ v.
v. 实施；执行
例句：We need to implement new policies.
雅思写作：The government should implement stricter environmental regulations.
contribute
/kənˈtʁɪbjuːt/ v.
v. 贡献；有助于
例句：Exercise contributes to better health.
雅思写作：Education contributes significantly to social mobility.
controversial
/kənˈtroʊvərʃl/ adj.
adj. 有争议的；引起争论的
例句：It's a controversial topic.
雅思写作：Genetic modification remains a highly controversial issue.
inevitable
/ɪnˈɛvɪtəbl/ adj.
adj. 不可避免的；必然的
例句：Change is inevitable.
雅思写作：The decline of traditional industries is an inevitable result of globalization.
deteriorate
/ˌdiːtɛriˈɔːreɪt/ v.
v. 恶化；变坏
例句：Relations deteriorated over time.
雅思写作：Environmental quality has deteriorated rapidly in urban areas.
prominent
/ˈprɑːmɪnt/ adj.
adj. 杰出的；显著的；突出的
例句：He is a prominent leader.
雅思写作：This is a prominent feature in the argument.
undermine
/ˌʌndərˈmaɪn/ v.
v. 削弱；破坏
例句：The leak undermined trust.
雅思写作：Climate change policies may undermine economic growth.
prioritize
/praɪˈɔːrɪtaɪz/ v.
v. 优先处理
例句：Healthcare must be prioritized.
雅思写作：Societies should prioritize education over luxury spending.
enhance
/ɪnˈhɑːns/ v.
v. 增强；提高
例句：Training will enhance performance.
雅思写作：Tech innovation enhances productivity and efficiency.
alleviate
/əˈliːvieɪt/ v.
v. 减轻；缓解
例句：This medicine helps alleviate pain.
雅思写作：Welfare programs help alleviate poverty in developing nations.
correlate
/ˈkɔːrləˌteɪt/ v.
v. 与...相关；有联系
例句：Stress correlates with poor sleep.
雅思写作：Research shows high correlation between education and income.
obsolete
/ˈɑːbsəliːt/ adj.
adj. 过时的；淘汰的
例句：Old technology becomes obsolete.
雅思写作：Traditional media becomes obsolete as digital platforms rise.
substantial
/ˈsʌbstənʃl/ adj.
adj. 大量的；实质性的
例句：They made substantial progress.
雅思写作：Substantial investment is needed to solve this problem.
mitigate
/ˈmɪtɪgeɪt/ v.
v. 减轻；缓和
例句：Measures can mitigate the damage.
雅思写作：Renewable energy helps mitigate climate change effects.

💡 实用短语 (Useful Phrases)

in conclusion
总之；综上所述
In conclusion, the evidence supports my argument.
on the contrary
恰恰相反
On the contrary, this proves my hypothesis wrong.
It is widely acknowledged that...
众所周知……
It is widely acknowledged that education improves outcomes.
from a... perspective
从……的角度来看
From an economic perspective, this seems beneficial.
to what extent
在多大程度上
To what extent do you agree with this view?
